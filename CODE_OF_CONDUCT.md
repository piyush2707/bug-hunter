# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, patient, and specific. Critique ideas and code, not people. Accept feedback gracefully and focus on what benefits the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, doxxing, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards fairly. Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers.
